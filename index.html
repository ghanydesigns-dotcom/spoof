The assistants

Two plugins, one job

Each one lives in a panel inside the Adobe app you already use. Pick a tool, set it once, and let it repeat the boring part.

G.A.R.V.I.S

Ghany's JARVIS, for Photoshop

V2.2.1Photoshop 23.3+Windows & macOSFree
  • ResizeCrop, expand or fit to square, landscape or portrait ratios. Expand fills the new area with Firefly.
  • Upscale2X to 8X with an enhance pass tuned for People, Landscape, Food or Product.
  • CreateReady social files (post, carousel, story) and CMYK print files with margins and guides.
  • ColorDefault and saved looks, capture your own from reference photos, share presets, import or export .cube LUTs.
  • Quick promptsOne-click prompts such as Day to Night, 3D to Real and sketches.
  • ExportLayers, artboards or layer comps to PNG, JPG, PSD, TIFF or BMP, plus PDF.
  • Clean upDelete empty layers, flatten layer effects and flatten masks, one click each.
  • BatchRun any tool on a whole folder and save as JPEG, PNG or PSD.
  • Quick barAgain repeats the last tool. Arrange, flip, rotate, group, bake generative layers and smart object convert, always one click away.

M.O.N.D.A.Y

Ghany's FRIDAY, for Illustrator

V0.8.1Illustrator 2020+Free
  • TextEdit many text frames at once, add prefixes, number sequences, align to baseline, paste and keep styles.
  • MosaicArrange selected images into a grid with set columns and spacing.
  • MoodboardFit selected photos of mixed sizes and orientations onto an artboard.
  • CreateNew files at social and print sizes with as many artboards as you need.
  • ArtboardsCarousel rows at Instagram and LinkedIn sizes, and place photos into artboards masked to fit.
  • ExportEvery artboard as PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF, named your way.
  • PrintOutline text, embed images, convert to CMYK and save .ai and PDF, for one file or a whole folder.
  • TraceImage Trace presets for logo, line art and photo, with a clean-up pass.
  • RecolorMap selected artwork to the nearest swatch of a saved brand palette.
  • Clean upRemove unused swatches, empty layers, stray points and hidden objects, with a report.
